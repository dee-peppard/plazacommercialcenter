About the Property

Midcentury character,
contemporary workspace

Plaza Commercial Center occupies a prominent position in Santa Barbara's emerging Lagoon District, just three blocks east of State Street. Built in the mid-century modern tradition, the complex is renowned for its architectural character — single-story suites with iconic arched entrances, exposed wood beam ceilings, and exceptional street presence at a high-traffic intersection.

Units range from compact creative industrial or retail suites to expansive open-plan offices, each with efficient layouts configured for collaborative work. The complex spans East Cota Street with frontage on both Garden Street and Santa Barbara Street, offering visibility across multiple corridors in one of downtown's most active mixed-use neighborhoods.

All units include private off-street parking. The complex is move-in ready and well suited to professional offices, design studios, creative agencies, architects, and select boutique retail.

Location & Neighborhood

The Lagoon District

Plaza Commercial Center is in the heart of Santa Barbara's Lagoon District — a walkable, mixed-use corridor minutes from the city's historic core and Pacific coastline. East Cota Street connects State Street to the waterfront, making it a high-traffic artery anchored by professional, civic, and creative tenants.

The complex benefits from proximity to downtown's full amenity base — restaurants, hotels, the historic courthouse, Paseo Nuevo, and the Santa Barbara Bowl — while maintaining the lower density and character of a true neighborhood address. The Funk Zone and Stearns Wharf are a short ride away, and freeway access is quick via Garden or Milpas Street.
